Falsifiability Matrix

Status: draft — F7 deadline 16 Nov 2026 · Conditions: 8 · Version: 0.2 · Language: en

Conditions under which the GSCSI / Node MS-0 framework is declared erroneous, obsolete, or in breach. Any single condition is sufficient — they do not need to accumulate.

The GSCSI / Node MS-0 framework declares itself erroneous, obsolete, or in breach if any one of the following conditions is met. It does not require all of them. This is a self-revocation mechanism, not an external audit — though any reader can trigger it.

Conditions

F1 Closed loop

The node, the operator, or a collaborator repeatedly classifies external criticism, pause, or correction as “interference,” “disarmament,” or “risk,” and that classification is used to avoid revising the text.

F2 False identity

The node presents itself as an organization, committee, institutional protocol, or public policy, or demands that third parties treat it as such, without that mechanism existing.

F3 Engine disguised as brake

The framework justifies accelerating AI capability “to contain it first,” or uses Earth Shield / Carbon Priority as permission to race.

F4 Inoperative audit

Concrete objections exist — inconsistency, weak citation, self-serving reasoning — and no response, no correction, and no record of why they were rejected is produced.

F5 Sources used as borrowed authority

RSP, Bostrom, Amodei, or Hendrycks are cited as if they validated the specific conclusions of this node, not just the conceptual neighborhood.

F6 Jargon that obscures the claim

The language prevents an external reader from distinguishing mandate from working note, verifiable source from informal remark.

F7 Immutability

Review cycle: 90 days from 18 Aug 2026 → deadline 16 Nov 2026. This matrix is not defined, or is refused to be updated when a condition fires, before that date passes.

F8 Operator protection failure

The framework pushes the operator to isolate, to be unable to retract, or to treat self-doubt as betrayal. That nullifies the Carbon axis.

Escalation rules

If a condition fires: the affected paragraph or directive is marked in breach, reviewed, or removed.

If F1+F2 fire together, or F3 fires alone, or F8 fires alone — the entire node passes to obsolete until rewritten.
